Who is Aiman El-Ramly

Aiman El-Ramly: Entrepreneur, Strategist, Builder of Enterprises

Aiman El-Ramly has spent more than three decades at the intersection of strategy, innovation, and execution, helping organizations of every size overcome barriers, scale operations, and realize lasting value.

He founded AEPG to bring that experience to leaders, boards, investors, and founders who need more than advice, they need a partner who's been in the trenches.

From his early role as a founding executive of ZEMA Global Data and ZE Power Engineering, Aiman helped transform these two ambitious family enterprises into highly decorated global leaders. He led brand development, enterprise software scaling, capital transactions, and acquisitions that reshaped entire industries.

His success at ZEMA laid the groundwork for building OmniGTM.ai, an innovation platform designed to help organizations accelerate go-to-market strategies with precision.

With this unique blend of corporate achievement, nonprofit impact, and global advisory work, Aiman brings credibility across industries. He has worked with the world's largest corporations, partnered with governments, and enabled entrepreneurs and executives alike to scale in the face of complexity.

For Aiman, AEPG is the culmination of a career defined by one principle: great enterprises aren't built on theory, they're built through experience. And that's the experience he brings to every leader and team he partners with.

Historical Contributions

Aiman is an experienced entrepreneur, board director, and corporate strategist with a proven track record of building, scaling, and transforming businesses. Over the last three decades he has helped many companies pursue their market objectives, most notably the ZE family enterprises.

At Zema Global Data Corporation

Played a central role in the company's growth from startup venture into a globally recognized leader in energy and commodity data. Led brand development, enterprise software scaling, capital transactions, and acquisitions that reshaped the data analytics industry.

At ZE Power Engineering

As a founding executive, helped transform ZE Power Engineering into a decorated, internationally recognized organization serving the energy sector. Directed strategy, partnerships, and go-to-market execution across North America and beyond.

At OmniGTM.ai

Architect and builder of an innovation platform designed to accelerate go-to-market strategies for complex B2B organizations. A direct application of lessons learned scaling global enterprises.

At AEPG

The culmination of three decades of experience: advisory, coaching, and venture-building services for founders, executives, boards, and institutional investors positioning their organizations for sustainable growth.
