Discovery Intensive

A 30-minute conversation worth more than most pitch decks.

Thirty minutes, senior partner, no slides. Most Discovery conversations start the same way: a founder, CEO, or board chair describes the thing keeping them up. We ask the two or three questions that usually sharpen the picture, and by minute twenty-five we are sketching the outlines of an answer.

If it is a fit for a deeper engagement, we will say so and propose a path. If it is not, you leave with a useful perspective and no pressure.

  • Prep: a short email exchange so we come in with context, not cold.
  • Call: 30 minutes, video or phone, senior partner only.
  • Follow-up: a one-page summary of what we heard and what we would do next.

What we will not do

No prepared decks, no sales sequence, no "let me loop in my colleague." If we are the wrong fit we will tell you, and usually we know someone who is the right fit.
